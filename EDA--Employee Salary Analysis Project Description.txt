		==============================================
			EDA--Employee Salary Analysis Project Description
		==============================================
An Exploratory Data Analysis of the dataset of San Francisco Employee Compensation for Fiscal Year 2014-15 obtained from Agency and data analysis was performed . After extensive cleaning, filtering and manipulation using Python, Pansdas and Numpy and the dataset was reduced to 83946 observations and 18 variables to probe into the statistics and draw insightful information u. The results of analysis are presented graphically for better data-visualization using Tableau.
